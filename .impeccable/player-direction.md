# Approved TV interface direction

THESIS: Keep the Sky-inspired blue app shell; let the video lead during playback through compact Netflix/Disney+ inspired bottom controls.

OWN-WORLD: Midnight blue, translucent navy surfaces, readable white text, pale cyan focus. Native TV typography and D-pad interaction. No continuous full-screen blur.

STORY: Choose a household profile, browse shelves or a seamless grid, open details, then watch. Account status is visible beside navigation and first in Settings. Restrictions explain recovery; reporting is reserved for structural failures.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Floating navigation, account badge and prominent artwork inside adjustable safe margins. Video and background artwork may bleed to the screen edges. Guest capacity is a service restriction, not a client defect.

FORM: Default 5% horizontal/vertical margins, device-wide adjustment from 0–10%. A bottom gradient carries a compact title, focusable seek timeline, elapsed/remaining time and icon transport. A scrollable one-third-width drawer contains vertical quality choices, audio and subtitles. Captions occupy their own safe viewport above controls. Back cancels a seek, closes settings, hides controls, then exits. Quality remains profile-specific.

Verification: 720p and 1080p emulator captures use public sample media and explicit QA fixtures when guest service capacity prevents browsing. These establish layout and interaction, not production service or physical-device acceptance. Physical ONN/Chromecast tests remain required.
