# TV interface

Mode: Experience, with Operate conventions for search and account settings.

Dark midnight background (#080C16) for living-room viewing. Off-white foreground, restrained cyan focus/accent (#76D7E4). Large backdrop artwork, readable title and synopsis, horizontal poster shelves, generous space between shelves. Compose TV Material components own focus, buttons, and interaction. Roboto follows Android TV type conventions.

Top navigation: Home, Movies, TV Shows, Search, My List, Account. Poster focus visibly scales and brightens; focus restores when returning from detail. Screens use lazy lists and stable title/episode keys. Search uses Android IME; account offers QR pairing and email/password fallback. Player uses Media3 native controller, captions, quality and audio menus.

Focus follows remote interaction. New navigation destinations focus their active tab. Detail entry focuses Play when media is available, or Back during loading, errors, and empty series. Home restoration identifies both shelf and title, with an independent hero target; returning from detail preserves that origin instead of refocusing navigation. Detail shows the synopsis once beneath the hero. Playback Back closes the quality menu first, hides visible player controls next, then returns to detail.

Validation scope: Home, Movies, and Detail captures reviewed at 1920×1080. The updated Detail capture confirms visible cyan Play focus and a single synopsis. Shelf restoration and player Back order reviewed in source. Screenshot review establishes visual readability and layout, not physical remote or playback compatibility. VIP authentication and playback on physical ONN/Chromecast hardware remain unverified.
